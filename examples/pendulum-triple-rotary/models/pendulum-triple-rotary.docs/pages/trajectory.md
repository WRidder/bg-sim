# Trajectory optimisation

The swing-up trajectory is computed offline by **direct collocation** (`design/triple_lib.py:collocate`, CasADi with IPOPT) on the same Lagrangian model, including the motor and its voltage limit. State $x = (q, \dot q)$, input $u$ (volts, piecewise linear between the knots).

$$\min \int_0^{T_s} u^2 + 0.01\,\dot u^2 + 0.05\,|\dot q_{2:4}|^2\, dt$$

subject to, on every interval $[t_k, t_{k+1}]$ of length $h$ (separated **Hermite-Simpson**):

- the dynamics as implicit constraints at every knot and midpoint, $M(q_k)\,a_k = \mathrm{rhs}(q_k, \dot q_k, u_k)$ with the accelerations $a_k$ as decision variables (no symbolic inverse of $M$: the problem stays sparse and small);
- $x_c = \tfrac12 (x_k + x_{k+1}) + \tfrac h8 (f_k - f_{k+1})$ and $x_{k+1} - x_k = \tfrac h6 (f_k + 4 f_c + f_{k+1})$, $f = (\dot q, a)$;
- hanging at rest at the start, $x(0) = (0, \pi, 0, 0; 0)$, **upright at rest** at the end, $x(T_s) = 0$, $u = 0$ at both ends;
- $|u| \le u_{max}$ and $|q_1| \le 2.5$ rad (arm travel).

The $\dot u^2$ term (input-rate regularisation) and the link-rate term select a smooth, slow trajectory among the many local optima; without them IPOPT returns bang-bang, whip-like solutions with link rates of 30–60 rad/s that no feedback tracks. Even with them the basin depends on the start: the first solve therefore uses a heavy link-rate weight (1.0) to pick a smooth basin, and the next solves relax it to the 0.05 of the cost above.

**Continuation and mesh refinement**, each solve warm-started from the previous one: 100 intervals, 15 V, link-rate weight 1 (135 iterations, 62 s), 100 intervals, 15 V, link-rate weight 0.05 (12 iterations, 6 s), 250 intervals, 15 V, link-rate weight 0.05 (29 iterations, 36 s), 250 intervals, 14 V, link-rate weight 0.05 (15 iterations, 19 s); 124 s in all. The first guess is link 1 turned linearly from hanging to upright. The last solve tightens the feed-forward voltage to 14 V, so the feedback keeps 1 V of the 15 V amplifier. The result: $T_s = 4$ s, link rates up to 11.6 rad/s, arm rate up to 4.4 rad/s, arm angle up to 2.50 rad.

The trajectory, its voltage and the gain schedule are written into [Ctrl](block:Ctrl) as the constant table `Tab` (one row every 10 ms, 401 rows: $x^*$, $u^*$ and $K$), generated by `design/build_model.py`.
