bg-sim games

Four games on models built in bg-sim, and a robot demo waiting for its model. Everything runs in your browser, with nothing to install.

  • Island Sortie

    A fighter, a mountain island and fifteen gates.

  • Short Final

    Anyone can take off. Bring it back gently.

  • Island Lift

    One helicopter, and the places nothing else can land.

  • Apex & Dust

    Two electric cars, four roads, and the clock.

  • Microduck

    Pollen Robotics' learned walking policy, run in the browser and driven with a gamepad. A stand-in plant until the bg-sim model exports.
Best on a computer with a keyboard or gamepad.
