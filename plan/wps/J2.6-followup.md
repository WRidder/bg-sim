## 2. DCMotor as a composite (J2.6b): blocked on two things

1. **Composite parameters.** A textual composite is the text form of a model-JSON subsystem,
   and a subsystem has ports only (`@ports`/`@inputs`/`@outputs`). J2.6 therefore rejects
   `@parameters`/`@variables`/`@equations`/`@initial`/`@discrete`/`@events`/`@causality` in a
   composite with E001. DCMotor must keep `R, L, k_t, J, d` runtime-tunable at `Motor1/R`
   (decision D4), so its composite parameters have to be *aliased symbolically* into the
   internal components' parameters. `FlatParam.value` is a number and `_p_pairs` calls
   `_number`, so J2.6b needs an alias in `Flat`/lowering (or a decision that a composite
   parameter is folded, which would silently break tuning — not recommended).
2. **Observable signal paths.** The flat block's `i` and `w_rel` are recorded signals named
   `Motor1/i` and `Motor1/w_rel`. A composite exposes them as `Motor1/<child>/i`, so the
   references in `test/test_flatten.jl:750-751`, `test/test_simulate.jl:491,519,687` and the
   plan 9.1 goldens would change. Either add an alias/observed-signal mechanism or rename
   the consumers in the same change.

## 3. Known limitation of the spec model (not a J2.6 defect)

`GearBacklash`'s damping is applied only in contact, so the transmitted torque jumps by
`d*w` at the contact boundary. At a hard impact (`w ~ 0.2 rad/s`, a 2 rad/s drive through the
10:1 gear) the algebraic motor torque jumps by ~1 N*m at the event; the DAE error estimator
sees the jump in the algebraic variable and the solver collapses `dt` (E901 at
`t = 0.12698294`). The text composite and the equivalent model-JSON subsystem fail at
*exactly* the same time with the same retcode, so this is the model/DAE, not the composite
wiring. All J2.6 goldens use drives where the jump is small (0.3 rad/s or less), which is
enough to exercise gap -> contact -> gap with a 0.216 N*m peak torque. A future fix belongs to
the nonsmooth/event work: either a smooth contact blend over a small `d*w` band (the spec's
own note about switching chatter) or excluding algebraic jumps from the differential error
estimate.
