### 3. Sections 5.2, 5.4, 5.5, 5.6 — the exact rules
Follow them literally, including:
- instantiation order (constants first so sizes are fixed, then subsystems recursively with
  path prefixes, then parameter expression binding through the front end in a "defaults" scope,
  then connection equations, then junctions);
- unconnected inputs → E101 with the hint of 5.2.6; unconnected power ports → E101 with
  "connect it or add a source (Se/Sf, Fixed, Ground)"; unconnected outputs are fine;
- model-level balance E103 ranking blocks by local imbalance and naming the worst;
- bond signs (5.5): a bond X→Y gives `Y.e = e_b`, `Y.f = f_b`, `X.e = e_b`, `X.f = -f_b`;
- junction signs (5.4): `s_i = +1` when bond i points **into** the junction;
- wire nodes by union-find over `wire` connections, all in one domain, across = `e` for
  Electrical/Hydraulic and `f` for Translation/Rotation (per D8), through summed to zero;
- signal connections: `input = output`, two sources on one input → E102;
- domain adoption (5.6) with union-find over bonds and junction islands, `0 domains` →
  dimensionless Generic with **W205**, `1` → adopted, `>1` → E102 naming the two ports.
