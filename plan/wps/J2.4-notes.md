### 2. SCAP, exactly (plan 5.7)
1. fixed (Se, Sf, Ground-like) with propagation;
2. preferred integral causality on C and I with propagation;
3. arbitrary causality on the remaining free R ports with propagation.
Propagation: a 0-junction has exactly one bond imposing effort; a 1-junction exactly one
imposing flow; TF: effort in on one side means effort out on the other; GY: both sides are
effort-out or both effort-in.
Results:
- a storage element forced into derivative causality → **W201** (with the instance path);
- a conflict on a fixed element → **E106**;
- a loop of free elements needing an arbitrary choice → **W202**, message
  "algebraic loop through R1, R3".
Return, per bond/connection id, `{strokeAt: "from"|"to"}` where the causal stroke sits at the
end where effort is the **input** to that element. `model.check` exposes it as
`causality = Dict(connId => (strokeAt = "from"|"to",))`; the editor draws the stroke there.
